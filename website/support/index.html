How can we help?

Everything you need to get PasteList working smoothly on your Mac.

Quick start

  1. Launch PasteList. It will appear in your Mac’s menu bar.
  2. Copy text, a link, an image, a file, or a folder as usual.
  3. Press ⇧⌘V to open your clipboard history.
  4. Select an item to restore it to the clipboard and paste it.

Assistive Paste

Assistive Paste is an optional accessibility feature for people who have difficulty using a physical keyboard. It lets you select a clip and paste it using only a mouse, trackpad, head pointer, or another pointing device. macOS shows its PostEvent permission under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

PasteList does not monitor keyboard input or read or control another app’s interface. It sends only ⌘V after you choose an item. If you do not grant permission, the selected item stays copied for manual paste.

Permission is not working

  1. Open PasteList Settings → Accessibility and confirm the Assistive Paste status.
  2. Open System Settings and enable PasteList under Accessibility.
  3. Quit and reopen PasteList if macOS does not refresh the status immediately.

History and cleanup

Unpinned clipboard items are automatically limited by age and count. Pinned items remain until you unpin or delete them. Use Clear History inside PasteList to remove stored clipboard data immediately.
