Simple by design

Copy. Find. Paste.

No accounts, dashboards, or setup rituals. PasteList stays in your menu bar until you need it.

01

Copy normally

Use ⌘C in any app. PasteList quietly keeps your recent clipboard history on this Mac.

02

Press ⇧⌘V

Open your history at the pointer and find what you need without breaking your flow.

03

Choose and paste

Assistive Paste lets you complete the workflow using only a pointing device, or keep the item copied and press ⌘V yourself.

Built for real work

More than copied text.

PasteList preserves the formats you use every day and makes them easy to find again.

A

Every useful format

Text, URLs, rich text, images, files, and whole folders — restored in their native clipboard format.

B

Search and save

Search recent items, filter by type, and pin the clips you want to keep close.

C

Automatic cleanup

Recent history is kept manageable with local retention, while pinned items stay until you remove them.

Private by default

Your clipboard stays yours.

PasteList has no account, cloud sync, analytics, or network service. Clipboard history remains inside the app’s sandbox on your Mac.
